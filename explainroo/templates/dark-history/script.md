# The crusade destroyed by its own arrogance

> Sample content for the template. Replace it with your own story. Facts here (the Battle of Nicopolis, 25 September 1396, on the Danube): a crusader army led by King Sigismund of Hungary, with many French knights, marched against the Ottoman sultan Bayezid I, nicknamed Yildirim, "the Thunderbolt". Sigismund wanted his Hungarian and Wallachian troops, who knew the enemy, to take the front; the French nobles insisted on the honor of leading. They charged, broke the Ottoman skirmishers, and rode uphill into a line of sharpened stakes; Ottoman cavalry then struck and the crusaders were routed. Sigismund escaped by boat down the Danube. Bayezid had many of the prisoners executed. The details differ between chroniclers; the numbers are left out on purpose.
