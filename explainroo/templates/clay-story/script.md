# The Lantern Light

> Sample story for the template: an original short story with three characters (a grandfather, a girl, a traveler), so the pictures can show that each keeps the same age, height and clothes. Replace it with your own story. One scene called "story" holds the whole narration; plan-shots.mjs cuts it into pictures of 2 to 3 seconds, so no [#markers] are needed.

## story {transition=fade hold=1.2 lead=0.3}
Mira lived with her grandfather at the edge of a small village. Every evening, he lit a big brass lantern and hung it by the door. Mira asked him why. He said a light helps lost travelers find their way home. One stormy night, the wind blew the lantern out. Mira grabbed a candle and ran up the hill. She held it high in the dark. Soon, a tired traveler walked out of the rain. He thanked her with a warm smile. Grandfather hugged her tight. Now two lanterns glow by the door, one for the travelers, and one for Mira.
