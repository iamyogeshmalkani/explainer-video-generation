# Templates

Copy one with `scripts/new-video.sh <template> <name>` (it makes `videos/<name>`).

| Template | For | Visuals | Needs from you |
|---|---|---|---|
| `starter` | explainroo's own blank project | drawn shapes and text | nothing |
| `history-map` | places and dates: a satellite map, a flying camera, flags, year tags | drawn from free NASA and Natural Earth data | nothing (today's borders only) |
| `tech-explainer` | how software works: neon-green outlined boxes on charcoal, one idea at a time (a phone app, a server, a database, requests and responses) | drawn by the kit in `lib/kit.js`, no pictures needed | nothing |
| `book-summary` | a book in under a minute: a cover card, pictures with slow moves, optional subtitles, three numbered takeaways | pictures or clips you supply (or Pollinations, local or Perchance pictures) | the pictures or clips |
| `motivation` | stoic and motivational shorts: a quote that appears word by word with glowing key words, then paintings with one-word captions, over a dark cinematic music bed | paintings you supply (or Pollinations, local, Perchance) | the paintings |
| `sticker-pop` | popup explainers: flat colored scenes where stickers, paper notes, number chips and bold words pop in one after another with a click, scenes push each other away | AI sticker pictures cut out of a white background (Pollinations, local, Perchance), everything else drawn by `lib/pop.js` | the sticker pictures |
| `youtube-random-facts` | "Did you know?" tech fact shorts: the sticker-pop look, with a hook first (DID YOU KNOW?), one surprising fact, then "Now you know!"; includes 20 researched tech facts in `FACTS.md` | AI sticker pictures cut out of a white background (Pollinations, local, Perchance), everything else drawn by `lib/pop.js` | the sticker pictures |
| `dark-history` | cinematic history shorts: full-frame dark oil paintings with a slow push, a thud at every cut, embers, a hook line and two or three word italic captions with the spoken word lit yellow, over a dark cinematic music bed | paintings you supply (or Pollinations, local, Perchance) | the paintings |
| `dark-history-hindi` | the dark-history look in **Hindi**: Hindi narration, Devanagari captions and hook, `epic` war music; plus `scripts/series.mjs`, which makes **many videos at once** from one series file (`series/*.md`: a title, Hindi narration and an English painting prompt per beat) | paintings made for you by `series.mjs images` (local, Perchance and Cloudflare share one queue) | a series file |
| `founder-story` | founder and biography shorts: real, openly licensed photographs (found on Wikimedia Commons) with slow Ken Burns moves and soft dissolves, a name and year plate, one big number, one quote card, word-lit captions, an end card crediting the photographers, over an inspiring piano-and-strings music bed that builds | photographs found for you by `scripts/find-photos.mjs` (no AI pictures, no stickers) | nothing (you check every photo) |
| `documentary-founder-reel` | **Documentary Mini Founder Reel**: premium editorial mini-documentaries about a founder or company: hard cuts, full-bleed footage and photos, landscape archive on black, white / black / red typography cards, paper cut-outs, triptychs, one italic-serif number, a tiny word-by-word Helvetica-style caption with rare serif, red and CAPS emphasis; no music, no logo | photographs found for you by `scripts/find-photos.mjs`, or your own clips (`shotNN.mp4`) | nothing (you check every picture) |
| `man-motivation` | "what a man can reach" motivation shorts: 2 x 2 grids of warm sepia portraits that fade in one after another with a thin line on the seam, a gold "And yet" turn alone on black, dust, thuds, over a dark cinematic beat | real, openly licensed photographs found for you by `scripts/find-photos.mjs` (you check every photo) | nothing (you check every photo) |
| `what-if-map` | "What if ...?" map explainers: a dark satellite Earth with a camera that never stops, flag fills, pins, emoji, a rolling counter, ranking bars, a white podium scene and small outlined captions | drawn by `lib/kit.js` from free NASA and Natural Earth data | nothing |
| `clay-stopmotion` | claymation-style explainers, free: AI clay pictures that move like stop-motion | full-frame clay sets moved by a camera that steps and trembles (10 poses a second), plus clay characters cut out of a green background that pop, drop, hop, squash and breathe, flickering lamp light, clay-lettered title and captions | clay backdrops and puppets, made free on this Mac by `scripts/shot-images.mjs --local` (about 2 minutes a picture) |
| `illustrated-explainer` | vertical illustrated explainers built from a shot list: a picture every 3 to 5 seconds with a slow camera move, soft crossfades, a title card, date/place tags, one big number, word-lit caption pills; also writes a scene-by-scene `storyboard.md`; optional depth-map parallax (`scripts/depth.mjs`) | pictures made by `scripts/shot-images.mjs` (local, Perchance, Cloudflare, Pollinations) | the pictures |
| `story-shots` | "how it happened" stories: a storyboard of pictures or clips, lit captions, a title card | pictures or clips you supply (or AI pictures from local, Perchance, Cloudflare or Pollinations) | the pictures or clips |

Each template has a `README.md` (how to use) and a `TEMPLATE.md` (the style rules
for an AI agent).
