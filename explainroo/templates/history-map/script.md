# Where the name America comes from

> Facts: Wikipedia "Naming of the Americas". The 1507 Waldseemüller map is the earliest known use; the name comes from Amerigo Vespucci (generally accepted). Other theories exist and are not claimed here.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.5}
When did we start calling it America? [#asia] For years after Columbus, many Europeans thought these lands were part of Asia. [#waldsee] Then, in 1507, a German mapmaker named {Martin Waldseemüller|Martin Vahlt zay mooler} printed [#map] a world map. [#america] On it, he labeled South America "America", after the explorer [#vespucci] Amerigo Vespucci. [#dropped] Waldseemüller later dropped the name, and Spain rejected it for two centuries. [#mercator] But in 1538, Gerardus Mercator used "America" for both continents, and the name stuck.
