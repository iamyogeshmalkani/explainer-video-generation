# Pixar almost deleted Toy Story 2

> Sample content for the template. Replace it with one fact from FACTS.md (or your own). Facts here: in 1998 a delete command on Pixar's file servers started erasing the files of Toy Story 2; the studio's own backups had also been failing; the film was saved by a copy that technical director Galyn Susman had been keeping on her home computer; the film was later reworked and released in 1999.
