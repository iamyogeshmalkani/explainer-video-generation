# Steve Jobs: fired from the company he started

> Sample content for the template. Replace it with your own founder. Facts used (checked October 2026): Apple was founded on 1 April 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne; Paul Terrell's Byte Shop ordered 50 assembled Apple I computers; the Macintosh launched in January 1984; in 1985 John Sculley, the executive Jobs had recruited, won the board's support in a power struggle, Jobs lost his operating role and resigned in September 1985, aged 30, and founded NeXT; he bought Pixar from Lucasfilm in 1986 (the price is reported as $5 million or $10 million, so it is left out); Toy Story (1995) was the first feature film made entirely with computers; Apple announced on 20 December 1996 that it would buy NeXT for $429 million and Jobs came back, becoming interim CEO in 1997; the iMac came in 1998, the iPod in 2001 and the iPhone in 2007; in his 2005 Stanford speech he said "I didn't see it then, but it turned out that getting fired from Apple was the best thing that could've ever happened to me."; he died on 5 October 2011, aged 56.
