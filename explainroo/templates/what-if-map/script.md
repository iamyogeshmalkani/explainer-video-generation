# What if all Spanish countries united?

> Facts: 20 countries have Spanish as an official language (Spain, Equatorial Guinea, 18 in the Americas). Nearly 500 million native speakers (Instituto Cervantes). Combined GDP roughly 7 trillion dollars, which would rank third behind the US and China (IMF nominal GDP estimates; Germany is fourth). Check the numbers for the year you publish.
