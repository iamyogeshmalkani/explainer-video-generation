# What if India worked a 4-day week?

> Facts: the UK 2022 four-day-week trial and Iceland's 2015 to 2019 trials found output mostly held and burnout fell. About 9 in 10 Indian workers are in the informal sector (ILO, Periodic Labour Force Survey).

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if India worked just four days a week?[/loud] [#p1] Many Indians work six days. Indian workers put in some of the longest weeks in the world. [#p2] Trials in the {UK|U K} and Iceland found that when companies cut a day, most kept the same output, and workers reported less burnout. [#p3] But India's economy isn't mostly offices. About 9 in 10 workers are in the informal sector: farmers, street vendors, daily wagers. A four-day law would not reach them. [#p4] For office workers, it would be huge. Longer weekends, emptier roads, a boom in short trips and tourism. [#p5] Factories, shops and hospitals that must run every day would need more shifts. That could mean more hires. [#end] More rest, same pay, same output. That's the bet. So what if India worked a four-day week?
