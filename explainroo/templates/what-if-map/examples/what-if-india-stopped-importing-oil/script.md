# What if India stopped importing oil?

> Facts: India imports roughly 85% of the crude oil it uses (Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; the share has been 85 to 88% in recent years). Buses, trucks, bikes and cars run mostly on petrol and diesel made from crude. Check the share for the year you publish.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if India stopped buying oil from other countries?[/loud] [#share] India imports roughly [loud]eighty five percent[/loud] of the crude oil it uses. [#vehicles] Almost every bus, truck, bike and car runs on it. [#dry] If imports stopped, petrol pumps would run dry within weeks. [#trucks] Trucks that carry vegetables, milk and grain would slow down or stop. [#factories] Factories would cut production. [#flights] Flights would be cancelled. [#power] Even many power plants and cooking gas supplies would be hit. [#prices] Prices of everything would climb, because everything has to be moved from somewhere to somewhere. [#end] A single country's oil decides how your whole day moves. So what if India stopped importing oil?
