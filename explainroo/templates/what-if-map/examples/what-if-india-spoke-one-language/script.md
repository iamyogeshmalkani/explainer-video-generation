# What if all of India spoke one language?

> Facts: India has 22 scheduled (official) languages; the rupee note panel shows its value in 15 languages.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if all of India spoke just one language?[/loud] [#many] India has twenty-two official languages, and hundreds more spoken in homes, markets and villages. [#note] Even a rupee note prints its value in fifteen of them. [#simple] One language would make life simple. A farmer in Assam could talk to a doctor in Kerala. Exams, courts and apps would work the same everywhere. [#more] But a language is more than words. It carries songs, jokes, proverbs and stories. Lose the language, and those fade one grandmother at a time. [#whose] And whose language would it be? Even today, many states feel strongly about this question. [#relearn] Millions of people would also have to relearn how to read, write and do business, almost overnight. [#end] India's strength is that it speaks in many voices. So what if it spoke in just one?
