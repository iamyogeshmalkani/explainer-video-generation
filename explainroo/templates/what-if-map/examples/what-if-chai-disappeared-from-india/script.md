# What if chai disappeared from India?

> Facts: India drinks most of the tea it grows; over a million workers on Assam, Darjeeling and Nilgiri estates. Tea only became an everyday drink in the 20th century.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if {chai|chy} vanished from India overnight?[/loud] [#drink] India drinks most of the tea it grows. Every morning, hundreds of millions of cups are poured. [#stalls] Start with the stalls. Countless roadside {chai|chy} sellers outside offices, bus stands and railway stations would lose their only income. [#farms] Then the farms. Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris grow tea, and more than a million workers depend on the estates. [#mornings] Then the mornings. {Chai|Chy} is how Indians welcome guests, close deals and sit with friends. [#tapri] Without it, the tapri conversation, the office break and the long train wait would go quiet. [#century] And a century ago, most Indians didn't drink tea every day. It became India's drink in just a few generations. [#end] It's only a cup of tea, until it's gone. So what if {chai|chy} vanished from India?
