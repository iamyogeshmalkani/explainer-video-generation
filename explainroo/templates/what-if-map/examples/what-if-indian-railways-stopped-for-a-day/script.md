# What if every train in India stopped for one day?

> Facts: Indian Railways carries about 24 million passengers a day (Ministry of Railways; the figure depends on the year and on whether suburban trips are counted). Australia has about 27 million people (ABS). Check both for the year you publish.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if every train in India stopped for twenty four hours?[/loud] [#people] More than [loud]twenty million[/loud] people travel by train in India every day. [#aus] That's nearly the whole population of Australia, every single day. [#stop] If trains stopped, workers couldn't reach their offices. [#stations] Stations would fill with stranded travellers. [#exams] Students would miss exams. [#freight] Then the freight. Trains carry coal, grain, fertiliser and fuel. [#coal] Power plants depend on coal wagons arriving on time. [#hours] Within hours, supply chains would begin to stall across the country. [#end] The country runs on tracks. So what if every train in India stopped for twenty four hours?
