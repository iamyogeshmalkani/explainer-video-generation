# What if every Indian state was its own country?

> Facts: India has 28 states and 8 union territories. At independence in 1947 there were over 500 princely states (565 is the usual count). Travancore (mostly today's Kerala) announced in June 1947 that it would stay independent and signed the accession to India on 30 July 1947, about six weeks later (Travancore's Dewan C. P. Ramaswami Iyer; check the exact dates for the year you publish). Uttar Pradesh: about 241 million people (2024 estimate; Census 2011 gave 200 million). Brazil: about 212 million (UN World Population Prospects 2024). Maharashtra holds Mumbai, India's financial capital. Gurugram is in Haryana, next to Delhi. The map is the Survey of India outline. Numbers are approximate, check them for the year you publish.
