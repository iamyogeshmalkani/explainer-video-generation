# What if India had no public holidays for a year?

> Facts: India has three national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti). The central government lists about 17 gazetted holidays, states add more, and most workers get roughly 15 to 25 days off.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if India cancelled every public holiday for a whole year?[/loud] [#p1] India has just three national holidays. But with central and state lists together, most people get roughly 15 to 25 days off a year. [#p2] Cancel them, and the country gains three to five extra weeks of work. [#p3] But festivals don't wait for permission. Diwali, Eid, Holi, Christmas, Pongal. People would take leave, call in sick, or just not turn up. [#p4] Some shops would gain. Diwali is the biggest selling season of the year for many businesses. [#p5] Millions of migrant workers go home for festivals. They would have to choose between the job and the family. [#end] A public holiday is the one day the whole country rests together. So what if India had no public holidays for a year?
