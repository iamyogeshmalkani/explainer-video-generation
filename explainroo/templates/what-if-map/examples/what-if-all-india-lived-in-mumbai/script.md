# What if all of India lived in Mumbai?

> Facts: Mumbai city is about 603 square kilometres (BMC). India has about 1.4 billion people. 1.4 billion divided by 603 is about 2.3 million people per square kilometre, against about 20,000 in Mumbai today, so over 100 times more crowded. Approximate.

[loud]What if all one point four billion Indians lived in Mumbai?[/loud] [#area] Mumbai city covers only around six hundred square kilometres. It's already one of the most crowded cities on Earth. [#crowd] But put the whole country inside, and it would be over [loud]a hundred times[/loud] more crowded than today. Millions of people per square kilometre. [#nospace] There'd be no space for homes, roads or parks. [#trains] Local trains, already packed, would be impossible.
