# What if every vehicle in India went electric tomorrow?

> Facts: coal supplies a little over 70 percent of India's electricity (Ministry of Power; check the year). India imports about 85 percent of its crude oil.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if every vehicle in India turned electric overnight?[/loud] [#perfect] At first, it sounds perfect. City air would get much cleaner, and India would use far less imported oil. [#problem] But there's a big problem. Charging all those vehicles would need huge amounts of electricity, and much of India's power still comes from coal. [#chargers] There also wouldn't be enough chargers. [#pumps] Petrol pumps would close, and mechanics who only know petrol engines would lose their jobs. [#everything] The grid, the batteries, the jobs, everything would have to change at once. [#end] Cleaner roads sound great, but where would all that electricity come from? So what if every vehicle in India went electric tomorrow?
