# What if everyone in India lived to 100?

> Facts: average life expectancy in India is about 70 years (UN World Population Prospects). About 1 in 10 Indians is over 60 today (UNFPA India Ageing Report, roughly 10 to 11 percent).

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if every Indian lived to be 100?[/loud] [#p1] Today the average Indian lives to about 70. Add 30 years, and the whole country changes shape. [#p2] Right now about 1 in 10 Indians is over 60. If everyone reached 100, nearly 4 in 10 would be. [#p3] Retirement at 60 would mean 40 years of pension. Pension funds and provident funds built for 15 or 20 years would be stretched to breaking. [#p4] Hospitals would fill with long-term needs. Diabetes, arthritis, memory loss. Elder care would become one of India's biggest industries. [#p5] Homes would hold four generations. And young people would wait far longer for the jobs their grandparents never leave. [#end] A longer life is a gift, until a whole country has to plan for it. So what if every Indian lived to 100?
