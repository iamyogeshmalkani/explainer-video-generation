# What if UPI stopped working for one whole day?

> Facts: UPI handles billions of payments every month (NPCI publishes monthly volumes; check the latest). Figures are approximate.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if UPI stopped working for one whole day?[/loud] [#billions] Every month, {UPI|U P I} handles billions of payments, from big shops to a chai stall on the roadside. [#down] If it went down, millions of people would reach the counter and find they can't pay. [#cash] Most of us don't carry much cash anymore. [#atm] ATMs would get long queues. [#shops] Small shop owners would lose a full day of sales. [#bills] Bills, rent and online orders would stall. [#income] It wouldn't just be inconvenient. For many small businesses, one day without payments is a day without income. [#end] When did you last carry cash? So what if {UPI|U P I} stopped working for one whole day?
