# What if India's population doubled?

> Facts: India has about 1.4 billion people, the most of any country (UN World Population Prospects 2024).

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if India had two point eight billion people?[/loud] [#already] India already has more than one point four billion. Now double it. [#food] First, food. India would need to produce twice as much rice, wheat and vegetables, on the same amount of land. [#water] Then water, homes and jobs. [#cities] Cities would grow into one another until you can't tell where Delhi ends and the next city begins. [#power] Electricity demand would soar. [#roads] Roads, schools and hospitals would need to double just to keep up with today's standard. [#end] India is already the most populous country on Earth. So what if that number doubled?
