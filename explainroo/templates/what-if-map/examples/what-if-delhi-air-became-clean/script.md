# What if Delhi's air became clean overnight?

> Facts: Delhi's winter air quality often reaches the CPCB "severe" band (AQI above 400); the "good" band is 0 to 50. Comparing smog to smoking cigarettes is common but approximate.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if Delhi's air was perfectly clean tomorrow?[/loud] [#bad] Every winter, Delhi's air quality often goes many times above the safe limit. Breathing it is often compared to smoking cigarettes. [#clean] Clean air would change things fast. [#hospitals] Hospitals would see fewer cases of asthma and breathing problems. [#kids] Children could play outside again. [#schools] Schools would stay open in November. [#flights] Flights and trains wouldn't be delayed by smog. [#sky] And for the first time in months, people could see the sky, the stars and the Red Fort from far away. [#end] Clean air should be normal. So what if Delhi's air became clean tomorrow?
