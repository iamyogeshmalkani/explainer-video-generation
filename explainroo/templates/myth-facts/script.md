# How Hanuman got his name

> Sample content for the template. It retells the childhood of Hanuman as told in the Valmiki Ramayana (Uttara Kanda, chapters 35 and 36 in the Shastri translation). Every claim is checked in `facts.md`. Replace it with your own fact, and fill in a new `facts.md` before you make the voice.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.4}
[#s1] [loud]Hanuman got his name from a broken jaw.[/loud] [#s2] The Ramayana says that as a child, he saw the sun [#s3] and thought it was a fruit. [#s4] He rushed into the sky to eat it. [#s5] Indra, the king of the gods, [#s6] struck him with his thunderbolt. [#s7] The blow broke his jaw, and he fell onto a mountain. [#s8] The Sanskrit word for jaw is [loud]{hanu|haa-noo}[/loud]. [#s9] His father was {Vayu|vah-yoo}, the god of the wind. [#s10] Angry, he carried his son into a cave and stopped the wind. [#s11] Every living thing struggled to breathe. [#s12] So the gods went to Brahma, [#s13] who woke the boy, and the gods gave him blessings. [#s14] [loud]Indra named him Hanuman[/loud], for the jaw his thunderbolt broke.
