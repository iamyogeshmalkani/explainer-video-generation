# Fact check: How Hanuman got his name

Fill this in for every video **before** you run `voice`. One row per claim in `script.md` (every name, number, place,
relationship, quote and cause). Status is `verified` only if you read the claim in a **named primary text** (a verse
or chapter) and, where possible, a second reliable source. If versions differ, write the version the video follows.
`node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` fails while any row is not `verified` or has no source.

Primary text used: Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, chapters 35 and 36 (translation by Hari Prasad Shastri, on
wisdomlib.org). Chapter numbers differ between editions, so say "Uttara Kanda" when you speak or write about it.

| # | Claim in the script | Status | Source (text and place) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hanuman got his name from a broken jaw | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 36: Indra says the thunderbolt "shattered his jaw" so "this child shall be called Hanuman" | The text ties the name to the jaw. Scholars also read the name as "one with a (prominent) jaw", so the video says "got his name from", not "means" |
| 2 | As a child he saw the sun and thought it was a fruit | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35: "he imagined it to be a fruit and rushed towards it" | The script does not say "rising" sun; the text I read does not show it |
| 3 | Indra struck him with his thunderbolt | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35 and ch. 36 (Indra: "the thunderbolt ... shattered his jaw") | |
| 4 | The blow broke his jaw and he fell onto a mountain | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35: "the child fell on a mountain, in his fall breaking his left jaw" | Text says left jaw; the script says "jaw" |
| 5 | The Sanskrit word for jaw is hanu | verified | Sanskrit dictionaries (hanu = jaw); Puranic Encyclopaedia on wisdomlib.org | |
| 6 | His father was Vayu, the god of the wind | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35 (Vayu is the child's father; "Sadagati" is a name of Vayu) | Other traditions tell his birth differently (for example through Shiva); the video follows the Valmiki one |
| 7 | Angry, Vayu carried his son into a cave and stopped the wind | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35: Vayu "withdrew into a cave to which he bore his child" | Do **not** say he "pulled the air out of the world"; the text does not say that |
| 8 | Every living thing struggled to breathe | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35: creatures "deprived of their breath" | |
| 9 | The gods went to Brahma, who woke the boy | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 35 and 36 (Brahma revives the child; Vayu resumes) | Do **not** say the gods "begged for peace"; the text does not say that |
| 10 | The gods gave him blessings | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 36: Indra, Surya, Varuna, Yama, Kubera, Shiva, Vishvakarma and Brahma each grant a boon | Say "the gods", not "every god" |
| 11 | Indra named him Hanuman | verified | Uttara Kanda ch. 36 (Indra names the child) | |

Open questions for a human reviewer: none for this sample. A reader from the tradition should still watch the finished
video before it is posted.
