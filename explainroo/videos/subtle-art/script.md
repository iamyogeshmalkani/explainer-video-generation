# The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck: book summary

> Facts: Wikipedia "The Subtle Art of Not Giving a Fuck" (not fully sourced). Mark Manson, HarperOne, 13 September 2016; subtitle "A Counterintuitive Approach to Living a Good Life". It argues that life's struggles give it meaning and that constantly pursuing happiness is a worse approach; it reacts against self-help that preaches mindless positivity; it urges values you can control (punctuality, honesty, kindness) over ones you cannot (such as popularity, a "bad value"). Nine chapters from "Don't try" to "And then you die". New York Times list: 327 weeks as of mid-September 2024. Kirkus called it "a good yardstick by which self-improvement books should be measured". Described as a prominent example of swear words in book titles in the 2010s. Not used because the source did not support them: the "feedback loop" and "limited f*cks" ideas, the 20 million sales figure (podcast-sourced), and the unattributed claim that the crude language hides serious ideas. The takeaways are this video's own reading, not quotes. On screen the title is written with an asterisk; the narrator says the full title.
