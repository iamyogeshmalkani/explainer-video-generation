# History of India and Pakistan

> Facts checked against Wikipedia: Indus Valley Civilisation, Mughal Empire, British Raj, Partition of India, India-Pakistan relations. Death and displacement figures are estimates and are said as ranges.

## intro {transition=fade}
India and Pakistan share thousands of years of history. [#split] In 1947, one land became two countries.

## indus
The story begins in the Indus Valley, around {2600 BCE|twenty six hundred B C E}. [#cities] Its cities, {Harappa|Huh rap uh} and {Mohenjo-daro|Mo hen jo dah ro}, had planned streets and drains. [#modern] Both are in present-day Pakistan.

## mughal
In 1526, {Babur|Bah boor} founded the {Mughal|Moo gul} Empire. [#taj] Its emperors built the Taj Mahal, and ruled most of the subcontinent at its height. [#end] It formally ended in 1857, after a major revolt.

## british
Meanwhile, the East India Company gained power from 1757. [#crown] In 1858, the British Crown took over directly. [#raj] This period is called the British {Raj|Rahj}.

## movements
Opposition to British rule grew. [#congress] The Indian National Congress formed in 1885, [#league] and the All-India Muslim League in 1906. [#demand] Later, the League demanded a separate country for Muslims.

## partition
In 1947, British rule ended, and the land was divided. [#pk] Pakistan became independent on {August 14|August fourteenth}, [#in] and India on {August 15|August fifteenth}. [#line] The new border split {Punjab|Pun jahb} and Bengal. [#people] An estimated 12 to 20 million people were displaced, [#dead] and deaths are estimated between {200,000|two hundred thousand} and 2 million.

## conflicts
Kashmir became the main dispute. [#wars] The two countries fought wars in 1947, 1965, 1971 and 1999. [#bangladesh] The 1971 war ended with East Pakistan becoming Bangladesh.

## nuclear
In May 1998, both countries tested nuclear weapons. [#treaty] The 1960 Indus Waters Treaty set rules for sharing the Indus rivers. [#suspended] In April 2025, India suspended it, citing an attack in Kashmir.

## outro {hold=1.5}
The two countries share deep roots. [#open] The disputes that began in 1947 are still open today.
