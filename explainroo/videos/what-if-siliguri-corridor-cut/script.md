# What if the Siliguri Corridor was cut?

> Facts to check: the Siliguri Corridor is about 20 km wide at its narrowest (sources say 17 to 22 km); Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh border it and China's Chumbi Valley is close. Schematic map; the neck line is stylised.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.5}
[loud]One thin strip of land, about 20 kilometres wide, holds India together.[/loud] [#neck] The {Siliguri|Sili goo ree} Corridor, the "Chicken's Neck", links the northeast to the rest of India. [#sides] Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh sit on its sides, [#china] and China is not far. [#lines] Rail, roads, pipelines and power lines all pass through it. [#blocked] If it were blocked, the northeast's supplies would need much longer routes. [#built] That is why India has built up roads, rail links and forces around the corridor, [#routes] and is working on other routes through the region. [#watched] It's one of the most watched few kilometres on any map of Asia. [#end] Should India build a second route? Comment.
