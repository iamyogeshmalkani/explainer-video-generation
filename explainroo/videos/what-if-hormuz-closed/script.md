# What if the Strait of Hormuz closed?

> Facts to check before publishing: the Strait of Hormuz is about 33 km wide at its narrowest between Iran and Oman; about a fifth of world oil consumption and a large share of Qatar's LNG pass through it (US EIA); India imports about 85 percent of its crude; India's strategic petroleum reserve holds about 5.3 million tonnes, roughly 9 to 10 days of demand (commercial stocks add more); Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline and the UAE's Habshan-Fujairah pipeline bypass Hormuz but cannot replace all of it. A what-if, not a prediction. Routes and pipelines are stylised.
