# What if China blocked the Brahmaputra? Part 1: The river

> Facts to check before publishing: the Brahmaputra is one of India's biggest rivers, but the Ganga is India's longest, so the hook says "one of India's mightiest rivers". The Yarlung Tsangpo lower reaches (Great Bend, Medog) project was approved in December 2024 and its power is widely reported at about 60,000 megawatts (some sources say up to 70,000); Three Gorges is 22,500 megawatts. The river line on the map is stylised, not surveyed. Names by country: Yarlung Tsangpo (Tibet), Siang (Arunachal Pradesh), Brahmaputra (Assam), Jamuna and Meghna (Bangladesh). Map: NASA Blue Marble; India's outline Survey of India via datameet.
