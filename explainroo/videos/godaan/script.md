# गोदान: किताब का सार

> Facts: Wikipedia "Godaan" (the article is tagged as needing more citations, so figures are unsourced there) and Hindi study sources on Godan. Godan (1936) is Premchand's last completed novel, begun in 1932. Hori, a poor peasant, stands for the farming community. He buys a cow on credit (80 rupees, from Bhola); his brother Heera poisons it out of envy. Hori's debts mount, his son Gobar leaves for the city, the panchayat fines him, and to save his land from auction he marries off his daughter Rupa for 200 rupees. The novel ends with Hori's death from overwork. Critics read his death as the end of idealism. Left out because I could not verify them: the village name, the final godaan scene, the grandson detail.
