# What if China blocked the Brahmaputra? Part 2: Assam

> Facts to check before publishing: Assam floods most monsoons (Assam State Disaster Management Authority publishes yearly figures). A big dam can store water and release it later; the risk is about timing and volume, not "cutting off". India has asked for early warning and hydrological data from China; check the status of the data-sharing arrangement before you publish. The map is schematic.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.5}
[loud]Assam already floods every single year. Now add a dam upstream.[/loud] [#store] A big dam stores water and releases it later. [#dry] In the dry season, less may arrive. [#farms] Farms, fisheries and river ports feel it first. [#monsoon] In the monsoon, a sudden release of stored water could add a flood surge on top of normal rain. [#warning] That is why India wants early warning and data from China. Without it, officials are guessing. [#timing] The risk is less about "cutting off" and more about timing.
