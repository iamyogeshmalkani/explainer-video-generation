# Jack Ma: rejected 30 times, then Alibaba

> Facts used: Jack Ma was born in Hangzhou, China, in 1964. He failed the Chinese college entrance exam twice (1982 and 1983) before passing. He says Harvard turned him down ten times (2015 Charlie Rose interview). After college he applied for about 30 jobs and was rejected by all of them; at KFC 24 people applied, 23 were hired and he was the one turned away (his own account). In 1995 he visited the United States and saw the internet for the first time; his first web company, China Pages, started in 1995 and did not take off. In 1999 he gathered 17 others in his Hangzhou apartment and founded Alibaba (18 founders). In January 2000 a group led by SoftBank invested 20 million US dollars. In September 2014 Alibaba listed on the New York Stock Exchange and raised about 25 billion dollars including the over-allotment, the largest IPO ever at the time. The user asked for the failures first, with the count, then the success.
