# What if the sea rose 10 metres?

> Facts to check: the Sundarbans delta (India and Bangladesh) lies barely above sea level; Kolkata sits only a few metres above sea level; south Mumbai is largely reclaimed land. Ten metres is an extreme case, far beyond current projections for this century. The flooded areas on the map are schematic, not a flood model.
