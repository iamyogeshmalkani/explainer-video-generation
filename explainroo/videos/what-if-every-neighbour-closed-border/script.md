# What if every neighbour closed its border with India?

> Facts to check: India's land border is about 15,100 km; six land neighbours (Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar); coastline about 7,500 km; most trade by volume goes by sea. Schematic map; the border lines are stylised and show no claims.
