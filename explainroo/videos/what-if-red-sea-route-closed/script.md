# What if the Red Sea route closed for good?

> Facts to check: the Suez Canal and Red Sea carry about 12 percent of world trade and roughly 30 percent of container traffic; since late 2023 Houthi attacks pushed many carriers round the Cape of Good Hope, which adds roughly 10 days and thousands of kilometres to Asia-Europe voyages; the Indian Navy deployed warships to the Arabian Sea in 2023-24 to protect merchant ships. A what-if, not a prediction. Routes are stylised.
