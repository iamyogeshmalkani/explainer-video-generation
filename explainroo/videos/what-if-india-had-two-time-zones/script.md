# What if India had two time zones?

> Facts: India is about 2,900 km wide; sunrise in the northeast is almost two hours earlier than in Gujarat. Assam tea gardens keep "garden time", an hour ahead. Russia has eleven zones, China one.

## story {transition=cut hold=1.2}
[loud]What if India had two time zones?[/loud] [#one] Right now, the whole country runs on one clock. But India is nearly three thousand kilometres wide, and the sun rises almost two hours earlier in the northeast than in Gujarat. [#assam] In Assam, summer mornings are bright before five. Offices open hours after daylight. Tea gardens already run their own "garden time", an hour ahead. [#two] Two zones could mean more daylight at work, and some studies say lower electricity use. [#russia] Russia uses eleven zones. China, just as wide as India, uses one. [#costs] But there'd be costs. Train timetables get tricky at the border between zones. Flights, banks and exams would need new rules. [#danger] And on busy single-line tracks, a confused clock can be dangerous. [#end] One clock keeps a billion people on the same minute. So what if India had two time zones?
