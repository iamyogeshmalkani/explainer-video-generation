# What if the Strait of Malacca was blocked?

> Facts to check: the Strait of Malacca is about 800 km long and only about 2.7 km wide at its narrowest (Phillip Channel near Singapore); roughly a quarter of world traded goods and a large share of China's oil imports pass through it; the Andaman and Nicobar Islands are India's, with Indira Point about 150 km from Sumatra; the Sunda and Lombok straits are longer alternatives; China has built pipelines from Myanmar (Kyaukphyu) and the Gwadar port in Pakistan. A what-if, not a prediction. Routes are stylised.
