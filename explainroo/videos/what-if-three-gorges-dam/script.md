# What if the Three Gorges Dam collapsed?

> Facts to check before publishing (sources: China Three Gorges Corporation, Britannica, Wikipedia "Three Gorges Dam"): installed capacity 22,500 megawatts, the largest power station in the world; the wall is about 2,335 metres long; the reservoir is roughly 600 kilometres long; the Yangtze basin is home to roughly 400 million people (about a third of China); the river runs more than 1,500 kilometres from the dam to Shanghai. "A collapse is considered extremely unlikely" is a general statement: find a source for it or cut it. This is a what-if, not a prediction.
