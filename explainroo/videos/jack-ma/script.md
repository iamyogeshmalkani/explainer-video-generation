# Jack Ma: the teacher who built Alibaba

> Facts checked against Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Ma) and 2014 IPO news coverage.

## intro {transition=fade}
Jack Ma was born in {Hangzhou|Hahng joe}, China, in 1964. [#name] His birth name is {Ma Yun|Mah Yoon}. [#teacher] He started out as a teacher, and went on to found {Alibaba|Ali Baba}.

## english
Before university, he rode his bike to work as a tour guide. [#tour] For nine years, he showed foreign visitors around, [#english] so he could practice his English.

## exams
School was harder for him. [#first] He failed the college entrance exam in 1982, [#second] and again in 1983. [#third] In 1984, he got into {Hangzhou|Hahng joe} Normal University, to study English.

## rejected
After he graduated in 1988, [#number] he applied for 31 jobs, by his own count. [#kfc] Every one said no, even {KFC|K F C}. [#lecturer] He became an English lecturer at {Hangzhou|Hahng joe} Dianzi University.

## internet
In 1995, the city government sent him to the United States. [#search] There, he searched the internet for Chinese beer, and found nothing. [#site] So he built a simple web page about it. [#replies] Within hours, Chinese investors sent inquiries. [#pages] He then started China Pages, an online yellow pages for Chinese businesses.

## alibaba
In 1999, Ma and a group of friends started {Alibaba|Ali Baba} in his apartment. [#site] It was a website where businesses could trade with each other. [#money] By early 2000, Goldman Sachs and SoftBank had invested a combined {$25 million|twenty five million dollars}.

## growth
From 2003, he added [#taobao] {Taobao|Tao bao}, a shopping site, [#alipay] and {Alipay|Ali pay}, a payment service. [#ebay] When eBay offered to buy {Taobao|Tao bao}, he said no.

## ipo
In 2014, {Alibaba|Ali Baba} listed on the New York Stock Exchange. [#number] It raised over {$25 billion|twenty five billion dollars}, [#record] a record for a public offering at the time.

## later
In 2019, Ma handed the chairman role to Daniel Zhang. [#tokyo] Later, he spent time in Tokyo as an invited professor at the University of Tokyo.

## outro {hold=1.5}
Jack Ma failed exams, [#jobs] and was turned down for jobs. [#build] Yet he built {Alibaba|Ali Baba}, one of the biggest online businesses in the world.
