# चीन पीछे क्यों हटा? और भारत ने क्या खोया?

> China declared a unilateral ceasefire on 21 November 1962 and withdrew from most of the east (behind the McMahon line), keeping Aksai Chin. About 1,383 Indian soldiers were killed. Nehru died on 27 May 1964. India reorganised and expanded its army. The next big flare-up was Nathu La in 1967.
