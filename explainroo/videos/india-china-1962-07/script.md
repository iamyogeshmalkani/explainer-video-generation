# वह चौकी जहाँ से युद्ध की चिंगारी उठी

> In June 1962 India set up Dhola Post near the Thag La ridge on the Bhutan-Tibet-NEFA trijunction (east). China said it was on its side. Chinese troops surrounded the post in September 1962. On 12 October 1962 Nehru said the army had been ordered to clear Chinese forces from the territory.
