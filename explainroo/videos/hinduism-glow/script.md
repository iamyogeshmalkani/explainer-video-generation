# Hinduism in one minute

> Facts: Pew Research (about 1.2 billion Hindus in 2020, about 95 percent in India) and Britannica (no single founder, no single holy book).

## intro {transition=fade}
Hinduism is one of the world's major religions. [#people] Nearly 1.2 billion people follow it, [#india] about 95 percent of them in India.

## roots
Its roots go back thousands of years. [#founder] It has no single founder, [#book] and no single holy book.

## brahman
Many Hindus see one ultimate reality, called Brahman. [#forms] They worship it through many gods, such as [#vishnu] {Vishnu|Vish noo} and [#shiva] Shiva.

## ideas
Four ideas shape Hindu life. [#dharma] Dharma means duty. [#karma] Karma means every action has consequences. [#samsara] Samsara is the cycle of rebirth. [#moksha] {Moksha|Moke sha} is freedom from it.

## practice
[#puja] Worship, called {puja|poo jah}, happens at home and in temples. [#festivals] {Diwali|Dih wah lee} and {Holi|Hoe lee} are major festivals.

## outro {hold=1.5}
Hinduism is a diverse tradition, [#end] shaped over thousands of years.
