# What if the Himalayan glaciers melted in 10 years?

> Facts to check: the Hindu Kush Himalaya holds the largest ice store outside the polar regions (often called the Third Pole); the Indus relies most on glacier and snow melt, the Ganga more on monsoon rain and groundwater, the Brahmaputra on a mix (ICIMOD, Lancet Countdown, IPCC AR6). Melting in ten years is far faster than any scientific projection. Schematic map.
